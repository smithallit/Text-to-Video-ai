### About the Guide

The Inherited Property Resource Guide is designed to help heirs, executors, trustees, and families navigate the complex process of selling inherited and probate real estate. The guide covers:

- Understanding probate processes
- Roles and responsibilities of executors and administrators
- Property assessment and valuation
- Tax considerations including step-up in basis
- Repair vs. sell-as-is decisions
- Marketing strategies for inherited properties
- Working with attorneys, accountants, and other professionals
- Case studies and real-world examples
- Checklists, templates, and FAQs
